Decision-Support Prototype • Phase 1 MVP

Could this adaptation work in a new place?

ADAPTFIT evaluates whether nature-based climate interventions—such as urban rain gardens—can be safely and effectively transferred from a proven reference site (Location A) to a target site (Location B). By matching documented environmental criteria (soil infiltration rates, ground slope, annual rainfall ranges, space availability, and foundation setbacks) while tracking transfer risk, ADAPTFIT delivers explainable, transparent screening to guide local engineering surveys.

Statutory Disclaimer (PRD §13)

“ADAPTFIT is a preliminary, explainable decision-support prototype. It compares documented adaptation requirements with available environmental data for reference and target locations. Results depend on data coverage, spatial resolution and the quality of the requirements; they are not a guarantee of implementation success.”
